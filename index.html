Questions

Good to know

Is QRMade free?

Yes. Every feature shown in QRMade is free to use.

Does QRMade require signup?

No. Generate and download immediately, without an account.

Are my QR details uploaded?

No. QR codes are generated directly in your browser. Local history, if used, stays on this device.

Are QR codes permanent?

Static QR codes encode their data directly, so they keep working as long as the destination does.

Can I download SVG and PDF?

Yes. Every QR code supports PNG, SVG, and PDF downloads.
